Clinical decision support that works with the cable pulled.

Open-source tools for clinicians working without reliable power, internet or physician backup. Guideline-grounded, clinician-signed, and small enough to go anywhere.

Research prototype. Not for patient care. Simulated scenarios only. Enter no patient information.
