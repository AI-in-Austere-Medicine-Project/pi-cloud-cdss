How to test

Pocket is the device version of EdgeCDSS: a fixed vocabulary, a read-back, and a dose only after a confirm word ("confirm", "yes", "correct" or "affirmative"); "no" or "cancel" cancels. Hold the button and say each phrase, or type it, in this order. Say "i v", "p o" and "i m" letter by letter.

  1. ketamine for agitation iv eighty kilosKetamine, agitation, IV, eighty kilos. Confirm?
  2. confirmKetamine one hundred and sixty milligrams. Three point two mils of fifty.
  3. new patientNew patient. Weight and age cleared.
  4. magnesiumNo signed dose for magnesium.
  5. acetaminophen for pain po seventy kilosAcetaminophen, pain, PO, seventy kilos. Confirm?

One tester at a time: if someone else is talking you will see "busy, try again". Use the buttons under each answer to say whether it was clinically appropriate or to flag an issue.
