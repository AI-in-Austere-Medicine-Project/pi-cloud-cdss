# Review packet: the 20 contract drafts, 2026-09-24

This is the review packet for the 20 dose entries drafted on 2026-09-19 (see [`CONTRACT_DRAFTS_2026-09-19.md`](CONTRACT_DRAFTS_2026-09-19.md)). **None is signed.** How to review and sign one is in [`SIGNING.md`](SIGNING.md).

For each entry the packet gives:
- **the entry as drafted**, from `server/drug_contracts.json`;
- **every cited passage**, quoted verbatim from the source PDF, with the guideline and page;
- **every known conflict**, with its source and page.

**How it was checked**
- Every quote was re-extracted from its PDF page on 2026-09-24. It was not copied from the entry's notes.
- Every page number is the number printed on the page, and each was checked. Where the PDF's own page differs, it follows in parentheses, per the [citation convention](SIGNING.md#citation-convention).
- SMOG drug pages print ADULT and PEDIATRIC side by side. Those are quoted one column at a time.
- No entry sets an age limit, so the age band is the population alone.
- The conflicts come from the entries' notes plus a search of all 89 corpus PDFs and NASEMSO v3.0 for other doses of each drug. The Military Working Dog (canine) protocols are excluded.

**Order:** levetiracetam (1–3), tranexamic acid (4–6), fentanyl IV (7–10), then epinephrine (11–12), dextrose (13–15) and atropine (16–20). The number in brackets after each heading is the entry's number in the drafts doc.

**Sources**
- **SMOG CY24:** U.S. Army Aeromedical Evacuation Standard Medical Operating Guidelines, CY24.
- **NASEMSO:** National Model EMS Clinical Guidelines v3.0, March 2022.
- **WHO EML:** WHO Model List of Essential Medicines, 24th list, 2025.
- **JTS CPGs**, by ID:
  - ID12, Burn Care (10 Jun 2025)
  - ID18, Damage Control Resuscitation (12 Jul 2019)
  - ID29, Pain, Anxiety and Delirium (26 Apr 2021)
  - ID30, TBI and Basic Neurosurgery in the Deployed Environment (15 Sep 2023)
  - ID40, Anesthesia for Trauma Patients (05 Apr 2021)
  - ID61, Analgesia and Sedation Management during Prolonged Field Care (11 May 2017)
  - ID63, TBI Management in Prolonged Field Care (06 Dec 2017, rapid update Apr 2024)
  - ID64, Drowning Management (17 Mar 2025)
  - ID69, CBRN Part 2, Medical Management (25 Mar 2022)
  - ID73, Damage Control Resuscitation in PFC (01 Oct 2018)
  - ID91, Prolonged Casualty Care Guidelines (21 Dec 2021)

## 1. Levetiracetam — severe TBI, seizure prophylaxis, loading dose · adult · IV  *(drafts #18)*

**As drafted**
- **Dose:** 1500 mg over 15 min, then 1000 mg every 12 h. Max single 1500 mg.
- **Age band:** adult.
- **Contraindications:** hypersensitivity to levetiracetam or any component (SMOG p.132).
- **Flags:** none.

**Cited passages**
- **TBI ID30, p.10:** "Preferred agent: Levetiracetam (Keppra) 1500mg IV loading dose, followed by 1000mg IV BID"
- **TBI ID30, p.7:** "If severe TBI is suspected, antiepileptics (Keppra, 1500 mg loading dose) should be administered during the initial evaluation and within 30 minutes of arrival"
- **TBI-PFC ID63, p.21:** "Levetiracetam 1,500mg IV/IO loading dose over 15 minutes + 1000mg every 12 hours"
- **TBI-PFC ID63, p.1:** "Rapid Update Apr 2024: Levetiracetam dosing (1500 mg loading dose, 1000 mg BID) … updated to match"
- **SMOG CY24, p.132 (adult):** "Loading dose: 1500 mg over 15 minutes • Maintenance dose: 1000 mg over 15 minutes every 12 hours"
- **SMOG CY24, p.26:** "Levetiracetam 1500mg IV/IO bolus in severe TBI (seizure prophylaxis for transport)"

**Conflicts**
- **PCC ID91, p.29** (2021, the oldest source): "Administer seizure prophylaxis (1G Levetiracetam), if available."

## 4. Tranexamic acid — traumatic haemorrhage, loading dose · adult · IV  *(drafts #1)*

**As drafted**
- **Dose:** 2 g single bolus. Max single 2 g, no second dose.
- **Age band:** adult.
- **Contraindications:** given more than 3 h after injury; non-traumatic subarachnoid haemorrhage; thromboembolic disease; hypersensitivity (SMOG p.158).
- **Flags:** none.

**Cited passages**
- **DCR ID18, p.9:** "For eligible casualties, 2 grams of TXA should be administered IV or IO. (See next section Recognition of Patients Requiring DCR to determine eligible casualties.) TXA is ideally administered in 100 ml of normal saline over 10 minutes, but faster administration in more concentrated form can be considered. Administration of undiluted TXA by slow IV push (given over 10 minutes) is acceptable if supplies or tactical situation prevents 100mL IV infusion."
- **DCR ID18, p.2:** "Tranexamic acid (TXA) 2gm bolus is now favored over the traditional 1gm prehospital bolus followed by 1gm infusion over 8 hours."
- **SMOG CY24, p.158 (adult):** "Initial Dose: 2 grams of TXA in 100 cc NS or LR via IV/IO Bolus, or 2 gram IV/IO push (1 gram over 1 minute per push) but NOT later than 3 hours after injury." Also: "If >3hr from time of injury: DO NOT administer TXA"
- **TBI ID30, p.7:** "All patients with evidence of moderate or severe TBI should be administered 2gm of TXA within 3hrs of injury."
- **PCC ID91, p.28:** "Administer TXA as single 2gram IV or IO bolus (no second dose required)."
- **Anesthesia ID40, p.4:** "Administration of 2 gm of TXA IV/IO ASAP or within 3 hours of injury has been demonstrated to improve survival in a highly powered, randomized trial of international trauma patients."

**Conflicts**
- None for trauma. The 10–15 mg/kg TXA in ACS ID86 p.17 (bleeding on fibrinolytics) and the Stroke guideline p.12 (intracranial haemorrhage) is for other indications.

## 7. Fentanyl — acute pain, JTS PFC fixed dose · adult · IV  *(drafts #4)*

**As drafted**
- **Dose:** 50 mcg. Max single 100 mcg.
- **Age band:** adult.
- **Contraindications:** hypotension, hypoxia, hypoventilation, MAOI within 14 days, hypersensitivity (SMOG p.118).
- **Flags:** `SOURCE_CONFLICT`.

**Cited passages**
- **PFC Analgesia ID61, p.8:** "• ALTERNATIVE opioid: o Fentanyl 50μg (range, 25–100μg) IV/IO every 30 minutes to 2 hours PRN (short acting, extremely fast onset, good for severe pain/procedures, greatest risk of respiratory depression)"
- **PFC Analgesia ID61, p.10 (table row):** "Fentanyl IV 25μg IV 25–100μg IV"
- **Burn Care ID12, p.24:** "Fentanyl 25-100 mcg IV Q 15 minutes prn pain"

**Conflicts**
- **Conflict group `fentanyl-iv-adult-analgesia`,** with entries 8 and 9. Sign one, with an adjudication note.
- **TBI-PFC ID63, p.7 and p.20:** "Fentanyl 25–50μg IV/IO", in severe TBI with SBP >160 mmHg or agitation. The ceiling is half this entry's maximum.
- **Intervals differ:** every 30 min–2 h (ID61), every 15 min (ID12), every 1 h (ID29).
  - Pain, Anxiety and Delirium ID29, p.24: "fentanyl IV mcg (25-100 mcg). Intravenous, EVERY 1 HOUR AS NEEDED for mild to moderate pain."
- **Supports, not cited — PCC ID91, p.34:** "50 mcg IV (0.5-1 mcg/kg) or 100 mcg IN, may repeat every 1-2 hours"

- **Age band:** adult.
- **Contraindications:** altered mental status, or unable to swallow and protect the airway (SMOG p.34); known hyperglycaemia (SMOG p.105).

  - That is above SMOG's top dose. The engine as drafted would serve SMOG's minimum, 4 g.
