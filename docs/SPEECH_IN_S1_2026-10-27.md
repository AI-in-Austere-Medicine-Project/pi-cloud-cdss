## 2. Speech in

### The endpoint: `POST /transcribe`

- Takes the access token, and a 16 kHz, 16-bit mono PCM WAV body (`Content-Type: audio/wav`), at most `CDSS_TRANSCRIBE_MAX_S` seconds (default 30).
- Answers `{"text", "audio_ms", "transcribe_ms", "device"}`.
- `text` is whisper's segments joined as written. Numbers, units and words are not rewritten. A segment that is only a bracketed tag is dropped: base.en writes `[BLANK_AUDIO]` for silence, and that is not words. Brackets inside spoken words stay.
- Refuses, with the reason: not WAV, not mono, not 16-bit, not 16 kHz, empty (400); over the limit (413); no weights, no package, or a whisper failure (503).
- It does nothing else: no query, no session-log turn, no audio kept.

### The model: `server/stt.py`

- pywhispercpp 1.5.1 (whisper.cpp 1.8.5) built with CUDA for sm_87, `ggml-base.en.bin` from `CDSS_WHISPER_DIR` (default `server/data/whisper`).
- The model is always handed a file path that exists, so pywhispercpp never downloads. `/transcribe` opens no connection.
- Loaded and warmed at service start in a thread, as Kokoro is (`CDSS_WHISPER_WARMUP=0` turns it off). On the Jetson: on the GPU in 1.1 s.
- GPU unless `CDSS_WHISPER_DEVICE=cpu`. whisper.cpp says which backend it took only in its load log, so the log is read at file descriptor 2 for "using CUDA".
  - pywhispercpp's own redirect misses that log when `sys.stderr` has no file descriptor (under pytest, for one), and the GPU then read as `cpu`.
- `CDSS_WHISPER_THREADS` (default 4). One transcription at a time.
- `/status` has `speech_in`: `available`, `detail` (why not), `device`, `model`.

### The portal

- **HOLD TO TALK** sits beside SEND. It records while held (pointer, or Space/Enter on the button) and stops on release.
- The browser's audio is averaged down to 16 kHz and sent as a 16-bit mono WAV. The microphone is released after each recording.
- The transcript is put in the question box verbatim, after anything already typed. Nothing is sent. The note says "Check the words, then press SEND."
- The medic presses SEND. That query is logged with `input_mode: "voice"`; clearing the box resets it.
- A tap under 0.3 s, a denied microphone, nothing heard, or a failure each give a note and leave the box alone.
- When `/status` says speech in is unavailable, the button is disabled with the reason.

### Install: `server/tools/install_whisper.sh [python] [dir]`

- Downloads the weights and checks their sha256 (`a03779c8…6d002`, 147,964,211 bytes). Builds pywhispercpp from source in a clean environment: its `setup.py` passes every environment variable to CMake. About 20–40 minutes on the Jetson.
- **Found on the owner's install:** the build left each library's RUNPATH at pip's deleted build directory in `/tmp`. The import failed with `libwhisper.so.1: cannot open shared object file`, though the library sat beside the extension in site-packages. A path under `/tmp` is also one another user could recreate and plant a library in.
  - Fixed without a rebuild: RUNPATH set to `$ORIGIN` on `_pywhispercpp*.so`, `libwhisper.so*` and `libggml*.so*`, with patchelf 0.17.2 from a throwaway venv.
  - The script now does this after the build. Rerunning it on an installed package only redoes this step.
  - A gated test on the Jetson (`CDSS_TEST_WHISPER_GPU=1`) checks every library's RUNPATH is exactly `$ORIGIN` and that the real model runs on the GPU, with silence returning "". It passes.

## 3. Release to transcript, 20 spoken questions

**Method** (`tools/stt_latency_run.py`):
- The 20 batch 2 and 3 harness questions, spoken by Kokoro (`bm_george`), averaged to 16 kHz as the portal does: 3.5 to 11.7 s of audio, median 7.2 s.
- Each was posted to `/transcribe` on :8012 (this branch, the service venv), one at a time, after one warm-up. Timed from the request leaving to the transcript arriving: release to transcript less the browser's own downsample and encode, a few milliseconds.
- qwen2.5:3b (Ollama) and Kokoro (the live service) were resident on the GPU throughout.

| | Median | p90 | Max |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Release to transcript** | **293 ms** | **331 ms** | 412 ms |
| whisper itself (`transcribe_ms`) | 287 ms | 326 ms | 405 ms |
| Audio length | 7.2 s | 9.0 s | 11.7 s |
| Word error rate | 0.22 | 0.37 | 0.46 |

All 20 ran on the GPU. 1 of 20 transcripts was exact.

**Accuracy, read item by item.** The speaker is synthetic, so the error rate is not a medic's.
- **Kokoro's error, not whisper's:** Kokoro reads "IV" as "Roman four". whisper wrote what it heard ("Roman IV axis").
- **Formatting, not meaning:** "80KG", "2L", "BP78/40", "four" for "4".
- **Drug names wrong in 11 of 20:** "Cephasolin", "Cephasolendose", "cephas olin" (cefazolin, 4 items), "cepteriaxone", "ceftryaxone" (ceftriaxone, 3), "rockuronium", "artisanite" (artesunate), "propofil", and "mithersol amfascidation" for "midazolam for sedation".
- **Right:** every weight, age and blood pressure, and ketamine, midazolam (B2-8), epinephrine, calcium chloride and naloxone.
- **Other errors:** "R-6" for "RR 6", and "push those epinephrine" for "push dose".

That is the case for the medic reading before SEND, and the case for a next step owned by the owner. Two options:
- a drug vocabulary handed to whisper as its initial prompt;
- a larger model (small.en).

**Not measured:** what the server answers to the misheard drug names, and a human voice in the field.

## 4. Memory with all three resident

Live service (`842d765`, Kokoro on the GPU), qwen2.5:3b loaded in Ollama, and :8012 from this branch with whisper on the GPU. :8012 did not load a second Kokoro.

| State | Used | Available | Swap used |
|---|---|---|---|
| qwen + Kokoro (live), no :8012 | 4,775 MB | 2,770 MB | 1,269 MB |
| + :8012, whisper not loaded | 5,236 MB | 2,309 MB | 1,269 MB |
| + whisper loaded (one transcription) | 5,500 MB | 2,045 MB | 1,269 MB |
| All three, after a live `/speak` | 5,556 MB | 1,989 MB | 1,232 MB |
| All three, during a model answer (qwen reloaded at 8,192 context, 2.4 GB) | 6,034 MB | 1,511 MB | 1,231 MB |

- **whisper base.en costs about 0.26 GB** of system memory. The process's resident size grows 0.75 GB, most of it shared, file-backed CUDA library pages (`libggml-cuda` 60 MB, cuBLAS).
- In the service whisper adds that 0.26 GB, not the second server's 0.46 GB, which was its own copy of the app and Chroma.
- **No spill.** With all three resident:
  - Kokoro answered a live `/speak` on the GPU: `gpu_fallbacks` 0, `last_fallback` null.
  - A model answer ran on :8012 with qwen at 100% GPU.
  - whisper transcribed on the GPU before and after.
  - No CUDA or NvMap error was logged.
- **Swap** was 1.2–1.3 GB throughout, from before this work. It did not grow.
- For comparison (#191): qwen + edgecdss-v3 + Kokoro is about 8.1–8.8 GB, and spills.
